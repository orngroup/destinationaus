Bali

Island of the Gods

🏛️ Denpasar💰 Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) — 1 AUD ≈ 10,300 IDR🗣️ Bahasa Indonesia / Balinese🕐 UTC+8 (AWST same as Perth, AEST +2 behind)☀️ Best: Apr–Oct (Dry Season)

⭐ Why Sell This Destination

  • Australia's #1 international holiday destination — over 1.2 million Aussies visit annually
  • Visa on Arrival for Australian & NZ passport holders — USD 35 at the airport
  • Just 6 hours from Sydney, 3.5 hrs from Perth — Australia's closest beach escape
  • Spectacular value — luxury private pool villas from AUD 100–150/night
  • Perfect mix of beaches, temples, surfing, wellness and world-class dining
Perfect for

Couples, Families, Solo Travellers, Schoolies, Wellness Seekers, Surfers

Recommended Duration

7–12 nights recommended

🌍 Destination Overview

Bali is Australia's backyard paradise — the most visited international destination for Australians year after year. Ancient Hindu temples perch on clifftops, emerald rice terraces cascade down volcanic hillsides, and the Indian Ocean delivers world-class surf at Uluwatu and Canggu. From the cultural heart of Ubud to the beach clubs of Seminyak and the up-and-coming surf mecca of Canggu, Bali has reinvented itself as a destination for every type of traveller at every price point.

🗺️ Top Sights & Experiences

⛩️
Tanah Lot Temple

Iconic sea temple on a rock formation — unmissable at sunset with a cold Bintang.

🌿
Tegallalang Rice Terraces

UNESCO-listed emerald terraces near Ubud — iconic Bali photograph.

🏄
Uluwatu Cliff Temple & Surf

Clifftop temple, Kecak fire dance performances and Bali's best surf breaks below.

🏖️
Canggu Beach Cafés

Bali's hippest neighbourhood — great surf, incredible smoothie bowls, digital nomad vibes.

🌋
Mount Batur Sunrise Trek

Pre-dawn volcano hike — catch sunrise above the clouds over East Bali.

🐒
Sacred Monkey Forest

Ancient sanctuary in Ubud home to 700+ cheeky long-tailed macaques.

🍽️ Food & Dining

Nasi Goreng (fried rice), Satay, Mie Goreng (fried noodles), BBQ seafood on Jimbaran Beach, incredible smoothie bowls in Canggu. World-class café scene especially in Seminyak and Canggu — some of the best brunch spots you'll find anywhere. Great vegetarian and vegan options.

ℹ️ Practical Information

💱Currency

Exchange AUD to USD in Australia, then exchange to IDR at authorised money changers in Bali (much better rates). Avoid airport exchange. 1 AUD ≈ IDR 10,300.

🏥Health

No mandatory vaccines. Typhoid and Hepatitis A recommended. Dengue risk — use repellent. Comprehensive travel insurance essential — BIMC Hospital Kuta is the main expat/tourist hospital.

🔌Electricity

Type C & F (220V) — Australian plugs won't fit. Bring European-style adapter.

📱Internet

Excellent 4G. Telkomsel or XL SIM at airport from ~AUD 10 with generous data.

💰Tipping

Not mandatory but appreciated. AUD 2–5 for drivers, 10% in restaurants.

🚨Emergency

Police: 110 | Ambulance: 118 | BIMC Hospital Kuta: +62 361 761263

Thailand

The Land of Smiles

🏛️ Bangkok💰 Thai Baht (THB) — 1 AUD ≈ 22 THB🗣️ Thai🕐 UTC+7 (3 hrs behind AEST)☀️ Best: Nov–Apr

⭐ Why Sell This Destination

  • 60-day visa exemption for Australian & NZ passport holders — free and no paperwork
  • One of Australia's most popular international destinations — direct flights from all capitals
  • Exceptional value — luxury beach resorts from AUD 80–120/night
  • Bangkok, Phuket, Chiang Mai, Koh Samui, Krabi — incredible variety
  • World-class street food, Thai massage culture and beach clubs
Perfect for

Couples, Families, Solo Backpackers, Retirees, Foodies, Beach Lovers, Groups

🌍 Destination Overview

Thailand is Australia's great love affair with Southeast Asia. Bangkok's electric markets and ornate temples, the turquoise Andaman Sea and limestone karsts of Krabi and Phuket, the cool mountain culture of Chiang Mai, the island paradise of Koh Samui — Thailand effortlessly delivers every travel experience you could want. With 60-day visa-free entry, direct flights from all Australian capitals, and outstanding value for money, Thailand is the perfect destination for first-time Asia adventurers and seasoned travellers alike.

🗺️ Top Sights & Experiences

🏯
Grand Palace & Wat Phra Kaew

Bangkok's glittering royal palace complex housing the revered Emerald Buddha.

🏝️
Phi Phi Islands

Crystal turquoise waters and dramatic limestone cliffs — day trip or overnight from Phuket or Krabi.

🐘
Elephant Nature Park, Chiang Mai

Ethical elephant sanctuary — feed, bathe and walk alongside rescued elephants.

🌊
Maya Bay (The Beach)

Alex Garland's fictional paradise is real — now carefully managed and open again.

🛍️
Chatuchak Weekend Market

One of the world's largest markets — 15,000 stalls of everything imaginable.

🧗
Railay Beach, Krabi

Stunning limestone cliff-backed beach accessible only by boat — jaw-dropping.

🍽️ Food & Dining

Pad Thai, Green & Red Curry, Tom Yum, Som Tum (papaya salad), Mango Sticky Rice, Massaman Curry. Incredible street food — Yaowarat (Chinatown) is a must. Amazing fresh seafood in all coastal areas. Vegetarian options widely available. Budget AUD 5–8 at street food markets.

ℹ️ Practical Information

💱Currency

1 AUD ≈ THB 22. Exchange at SuperRich or Vasu exchange in Bangkok — much better rates than airport or ATMs. Wise card highly recommended to avoid fees. Budget AUD 80–150/day for comfortable travel.

🏥Health

Hepatitis A, Typhoid recommended. Dengue risk — use repellent. Australian Medicare not valid — travel insurance essential. Bumrungrad Hospital in Bangkok is world-class.

🔌Electricity

Type A, B & C (220V) — bring adapter. Australian plugs sometimes fit Type C sockets.

📱Internet

Excellent 4G. AIS tourist SIM from airport ~AUD 15 with 30GB.

💰Tipping

THB 20–50 for taxis, 10% in restaurants, THB 100+ for masseuses. More expected than in Australia.

🚨Emergency

Tourist Police: 1155 | Ambulance: 1669 | Police: 191

Japan

Land of the Rising Sun

🏛️ Tokyo💰 Japanese Yen (JPY) — 1 AUD ≈ 100 JPY🗣️ Japanese🕐 UTC+9 (1 hr behind AEST, 3 hrs behind AEDT)☀️ Best: Mar–May (Cherry Blossom) or Oct–Nov (Autumn)

⭐ Why Sell This Destination

  • Visa-free for Australian & NZ passport holders — 90 days
  • 1 AUD ≈ ¥100 — best value for Australians in over a decade
  • Cherry blossom season (late March–April) — bucket list experience
  • Direct flights from Sydney and Melbourne with Qantas and Japan Airlines
  • World-class food, bullet trains, extraordinary safety and culture
Perfect for

Couples, Families, Culture Enthusiasts, Foodies, First-Time Asia, Retirees

Recommended Duration

14–18 nights recommended

🌍 Destination Overview

Japan is the world's most fascinating destination and it has never been better value for Australians — with 1 AUD buying around ¥100, Japan is genuinely affordable for the first time in decades. Ancient temples and Shinto shrines sit alongside neon-lit megacities; the bullet train whisks you between Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka in hours; and every convenience store meal is better than most restaurants in other countries. Cherry blossom season in late March is one of the world's great travel spectacles.

🗺️ Top Sights & Experiences

🗻
Mt Fuji & Hakone

Japan's iconic volcano — day trip from Tokyo with spectacular views and hot springs.

⛩️
Fushimi Inari Shrine, Kyoto

Thousands of vermilion torii gates winding up a Kyoto mountainside.

🏙️
Shibuya Crossing, Tokyo

The world's busiest pedestrian crossing — an unforgettable sensory explosion.

🎋
Arashiyama Bamboo Grove

Magical towering bamboo forest in Kyoto — ethereal and completely unique.

🍜
Osaka Dotonbori

Japan's kitchen — neon-lit canal district for endless street food and nightlife.

☮️
Hiroshima Peace Memorial

Profoundly moving Peace Memorial Park — essential for all visitors to Japan.

🍽️ Food & Dining

Sushi, Ramen, Tempura, Wagyu beef, Yakitori, Matcha desserts, Okonomiyaki. Every convenience store (7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart) has incredible food. Budget ¥1,500–3,000 (~AUD 15–30) for a good restaurant meal. Street food from ¥300–500 (~AUD 3–5).

ℹ️ Practical Information

💱Currency

Japan is largely cash-based — withdraw JPY from 7-Eleven or Japan Post ATMs (they accept foreign cards). IC Card (Suica) essential for all trains, buses and convenience stores. 1 AUD ≈ ¥100 — excellent value currently.

🏥Health

No mandatory vaccines. World-class hospitals. Australian Medicare not valid — travel insurance essential.

🔌Electricity

Type A (100V) — Australian plugs won't fit. Buy a Type A adapter before leaving.

📱Internet

Excellent. Pocket Wi-Fi rental recommended (book online before travel). Airalo eSIM works well.

💰Tipping

NEVER TIP — it is considered rude in Japan. Service is built in. This includes restaurants, taxis, hotels.

🚨Emergency

Police: 110 | Ambulance/Fire: 119

Singapore

The Garden City

🏛️ Singapore💰 Singapore Dollar (SGD) — 1 AUD ≈ SGD 0.88🗣️ English / Mandarin / Malay / Tamil🕐 UTC+8 (2 hrs behind AEST)☀️ Best: Feb–Apr (Driest months)

⭐ Why Sell This Destination

  • Visa-free for Australian & NZ passport holders — 90 days
  • World's best airport (Jewel Changi) — the layover that became a destination
  • Just 7–8 hours from Sydney, 5 hours from Perth

Singapore is Asia's most polished destination and the world's most convenient stopover — nearly every Australian flight to Europe passes through, and Changi Airport's Jewel waterfall terminal makes even layovers an event. As a standalone destination, 4–6 nights reveals a gleaming city-state where futuristic Supertree gardens, world-class theme parks, and some of the planet's best street food await. English-speaking, safe, efficient and endlessly fascinating.

💧
Jewel Changi Airport

40m indoor Rain Vortex waterfall inside the world's best airport — free to visit.

🏊
Marina Bay Sands Skypark

The world's most photographed infinity pool — observation deck open to non-guests too.

🌿
Gardens by the Bay

Supertrees and glass biome conservatories — spectacular nightly light and sound show.

🍜
Hawker Centres

Maxwell Food Centre, Newton Circus — Michelin-starred street food from AUD 4.

🎢
Sentosa Island

Universal Studios, Adventure Cove Waterpark, cable car, S.E.A. Aquarium.

🛍️
Orchard Road Shopping

Singapore's famous shopping belt — ION, Paragon, Takashimaya, Somerset.

🍽️ Food & Dining

Hainanese Chicken Rice, Laksa, Char Kway Teow, Chilli Crab, Bak Kut Teh (pork rib soup), Kaya toast. Incredible value at hawker centres — full meal AUD 4–8. World-class fine dining too at Marina Bay Sands and the CBD. Halal certified options everywhere.

ℹ️ Practical Information

💱Currency

1 AUD ≈ SGD 0.88 — roughly equivalent. Cards accepted almost everywhere — Singapore is near-cashless. Changi Airport has excellent exchange rates. EZ-Link card for MRT and buses (AUD 12 from 7-Eleven).

🏥Health

No mandatory vaccines. World-class hospitals — but expensive. Travel insurance recommended. Tap water safe to drink.

🔌Electricity

Type G British plugs (230V) — same as UK. Australian plugs won't fit without adapter.

📱Internet

World-class 5G. Free Wi-Fi at MRT stations. Singtel/Starhub tourist SIMs from airport.

💰Tipping

Not expected — service charge included. Tipping not part of Singapore culture.

Police: 999 | Ambulance/Fire: 995
